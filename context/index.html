earmarkIQ Context is an MCP server in development. Once it ships, you'll be able to connect your earmarkIQ data to Claude and other AI assistants that support the standard — and ask about your own finances, grounded in your real numbers.

What's an MCP server, in plain terms?

MCP — the Model Context Protocol — is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to outside sources of information, with your permission. An MCP server is the bridge. earmarkIQ Context will be that bridge for your finances: your assistant gets permissioned, read-only access to your categorised earmarkIQ data, so instead of answering from general knowledge, it answers from your actual spending, subscriptions and net worth.

What it will let you do

"What did I spend on eating out last month?"
"Which of my subscriptions went up this year?"
"How has my net worth moved since January?"

Wherever you already talk to your assistant — desktop, IDE, chat app — your financial picture comes with you.

🔒 Read-only, by design

earmarkIQ Context can answer questions about your data — nothing more. It has no ability to move money, make payments or change anything on your accounts, and earmarkIQ itself uses read-only Open Banking access (AIS) via Finexer, an FCA-authorised provider.
