earmarkIQ Context is a live, read-only MCP server. Connect your earmarkIQ data to Claude — verified — or any MCP-capable assistant, and ask about your own finances with answers grounded in your real numbers, not general knowledge.

What's an MCP server, in plain terms?

MCP — the Model Context Protocol — is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to outside sources of information, with your permission. An MCP server is the bridge. earmarkIQ Context is that bridge for your finances: your assistant gets permissioned, read-only access to your categorised earmarkIQ data — collected through UK Open Banking and organised for you — so instead of answering from general knowledge, it answers from your actual position.

What your assistant can read

  • ✓Accounts and balances — every connected account in one picture
  • ✓Spending by category — categorised automatically by earmarkIQ
  • ✓Net worth and property equity — including your indexed property valuation and mortgage
  • ✓Subscriptions — recurring payments and price changes
  • ✓Your payday plan — the allocation earmarkIQ builds each payday
"What did I spend on eating out last month?"
"Which of my subscriptions went up this year?"
"How much equity do I have in the flat, and how has my net worth moved since January?"
🔒 Read-only, guaranteed

An assistant connected through Context can read your data — and that is all. It cannot move money, make payments, change settings, or alter anything, anywhere. You can revoke its access at any time, and revoking changes nothing else about your earmarkIQ account. The wider story of why read-only design matters is in connecting your financial data to AI assistants.

How to connect

Get your Context address

Your personal earmarkIQ Context address comes from your earmarkIQ account.

Add it as a custom connector

In any MCP-capable assistant, add earmarkIQ Context as a custom connector using that address. Claude is verified; other MCP-capable assistants connect the same way.

Approve read-only access

Your assistant will ask to connect — approve it, and it can start answering from your data. Your bank connection and credentials stay with earmarkIQ; the assistant never sees them.

To disconnect: revoke access from your earmarkIQ account, or remove the connector in your assistant. Either ends access immediately.

Questions

What is an MCP server? ›
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants such as Claude connect securely to outside sources of information, with your permission. An MCP server is the bridge: it gives your assistant permissioned access to specific data — in earmarkIQ Context's case, your own categorised financial picture — so answers are grounded in your real numbers rather than general knowledge.
Can an assistant connected via Context move my money? ›
No. earmarkIQ Context is read-only: an assistant can read your data, and that is all. It cannot move money, make payments, change settings, or alter anything on your accounts, and you can revoke its access at any time.
Which assistants work with it? ›
Any assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol and custom connectors. Claude is verified; because MCP is an open standard, other MCP-capable assistants can connect the same way, though we haven't verified each one individually.
How do I disconnect? ›
Revoke Context access from your earmarkIQ account at any time, or remove the connector from your assistant. Access ends there and then — nothing about your earmarkIQ app or bank connections is affected.
