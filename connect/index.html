Connect earmarkIQ to your AI assistant.

Ask Claude what you spent on groceries last month, which subscriptions crept up, or whether you can afford a holiday — answered from your real accounts rather than a guess. The connection reads. It cannot act.

How it works, without the jargon

earmarkIQ already holds a read-only Open Banking connection to your accounts. Context opens a second, narrower door on top of that: it lets an assistant you choose read the picture earmarkIQ has built, under scopes you grant one at a time.

Your bank is never involved in this second step. The assistant never sees your banking credentials, never talks to your bank, and cannot reach anything you have not granted.

What you need

An earmarkIQ account on Pro, with at least one bank connected, and an assistant that supports custom MCP connectors. Context is a Pro feature; it is not on the free tier or on Plus.

What your assistant can read

Five scopes, each granted and revoked on its own. Grant one, grant all five, or change your mind later.

What it cannot do

Context is read-only by construction rather than by policy. There is no tool in it that writes, moves or changes anything, because no such tool has been built — and a test fails the build if one is ever added. That is a stronger guarantee than a setting you could switch off by accident.

  • ✗It cannot move money. earmarkIQ connects to your bank under account information services only. Payment initiation is not enabled.
  • ✗It cannot change your earmarkIQ data. No budgets, no goals, no categories, no edits of any kind.
  • ✗It cannot reach your bank. Your banking credentials never leave your bank, and the assistant never sees them.
  • ✗It cannot give you financial advice. earmarkIQ is not authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority. It shows you your own numbers; what you do with them is your decision.
What read-only does not cover

Read-only describes what Context can do to your data. It says nothing about what happens to the conversation once your data is inside the assistant. Whatever you ask Claude, ChatGPT or any other assistant is handled under that provider's privacy terms, not ours — and may be retained or reviewed according to their policy, not this one.

Grant the narrowest scopes that answer your question, and read the assistant provider's own terms before connecting anything. We would rather say that plainly than let read-only sound like a privacy guarantee it is not.

The access log

Every request an assistant makes is recorded and readable by you in the app: which tool ran, which scope it used, and when. It is not a summary or a sample — it is the list.

That matters more than any promise on a marketing page. A read-only claim you cannot audit is a claim you are taking on trust; a log you can open yourself is a claim you can check. If you connect an assistant and later wonder what it looked at, the answer is already written down.

How to revoke it

Two routes, both immediate:

  • In earmarkIQ — open Connected assistants, and revoke the connection or narrow its scopes. It stops working on the next request.
  • In your assistant — remove the connector. earmarkIQ stops being called at all.

Revoking access to Context does not touch your bank connection or your earmarkIQ account. It only closes the door the assistant was reading through.

Where earmarkIQ sits

Assistants that can touch your finances fall into two camps. Some can act: tag spending, create rules, schedule a transfer for you to approve. Others can only read. Era, for instance, offers UK Open Banking through MCP with the ability to write as well as read.

earmarkIQ is firmly in the second camp, and that is the point rather than a gap in the roadmap. An assistant that cannot act cannot act wrongly. If you want something that moves money on your behalf, earmarkIQ is not it.

Questions

Can I connect my bank account to an AI assistant?▾
Not directly, and you should be wary of anything that offers to. Your bank has no way to give ChatGPT or Claude access to your account. What you can do is connect your bank to a regulated app under Open Banking, and then give an assistant read-only access to what that app has already gathered. That is how earmarkIQ Context works: earmarkIQ holds the bank connection, and the assistant reads a copy of the picture earmarkIQ built. The assistant never touches your bank and never sees your banking credentials.
Is it safe to give an AI assistant my bank data?▾
It depends on three things: whether the connection can only read, whether you can see what it read, and whether you can revoke it. earmarkIQ Context is read-only by construction, logs every access where you can read the log, and can be revoked from either end at any time. What it cannot control is what your assistant's provider does with the conversation afterwards, which is governed by their terms and not ours. Grant the narrowest scopes that answer your question.
What can the assistant actually see?▾
Only the scopes you grant, and there are five: spending, transactions, balances and assets, income, and subscriptions. Each is granted and revoked separately. Transaction history defaults to the last 12 months and is capped at 24 months, so an assistant cannot pull your entire history.
Can the assistant move my money?▾
No. There is no tool in Context that writes, moves or changes anything, and a test fails the build if one is added. earmarkIQ connects to your bank under account information services only; payment initiation is not enabled. Other products in this space do offer assistants that can act on your accounts with approval. earmarkIQ deliberately does not.
Which assistants does this work with?▾
Any assistant that supports custom MCP connectors. Claude is the one we have verified end to end. Others use the same open standard and are expected to work, but we have not verified each one individually, so we describe the connector address rather than invented menu paths.
Do I need a paid plan?▾
Yes. Context is a Pro feature. You also need at least one bank connected in earmarkIQ, because the assistant reads the picture earmarkIQ has built rather than talking to your bank.
