Property & Equity

Your home is most of
your net worth. Track it
like it counts.

Add your address and earmarkIQ keeps a running estimate of what your property is worth, moved each month by the official UK House Price Index. Put your mortgage balance in alongside it and your equity — value minus what you still owe — feeds straight into your net worth, without you touching a spreadsheet again.

01
Add your address
Search for your property and confirm it, then tell earmarkIQ what you paid and when. That purchase price is the starting point for everything that follows.
02
The estimate updates monthly
earmarkIQ takes the House Price Index movement for your local authority since the month you bought, and applies it to your purchase price. When HM Land Registry publishes the next monthly index, your figure moves with it.
03
Enter your mortgage balance
Add what is still outstanding on the mortgage and earmarkIQ works out your equity and your loan-to-value, so you can see the debt shrinking against the asset.
04
Equity flows into net worth
Property equity sits beside your current accounts, savings, investments and debts in a single net worth figure that keeps itself current. No monthly re-entry, no guessing.
This is an estimate, not a valuation

earmarkIQ applies published index movement to the price you paid. Nobody visits your property, and the figure is not a surveyor's valuation, a lender's valuation or an estate agent's appraisal. Extensions, condition and the particulars of your street are not in it. If you have a number you trust more, you can override the estimate with your own figure at any time and earmarkIQ will use that instead.

What Pro adds

Every tier gets the local-authority House Price Index estimate described above. Pro adds an address-level market valuation — a figure calculated for your specific property rather than for the whole local authority it sits in.

earmarkIQ Context — live

Ask your own AI
assistant about
your own money.

earmarkIQ Context is a read-only MCP server. Connect it to Claude — verified — or any MCP-capable assistant, and it can answer from your actual accounts instead of general knowledge. It can read. It cannot act. Not because we have promised not to, but because no capability to act exists inside it.

1
The permission it runs on is a reading permission
earmarkIQ connects to your bank under Account Information Services through Finexer Ltd (FRN 925695). AIS cannot construct a payment instruction, so there is nothing to reach even before Context is involved.
2
Context publishes reads only
An MCP server exposes a list of operations and an assistant can invoke what is on it, nothing else. Ours contains read operations and no others — there is no mutating call to trigger, or to be argued into.
3
A test fails the build if that ever changes
Guarantees erode through changes that seemed reasonable at the time. So it is enforced in the build: adding any mutating capability breaks it, visibly, and someone has to consciously delete the test to proceed.
Audited and revocable

Every access through Context is recorded, so “we would never” can be replaced with a record of what actually happened. You can revoke the connection yourself from your earmarkIQ account at any moment — immediately, without contacting anyone, and without affecting your bank connections or the app.

The web complements the iOS app rather than replacing it. Bank connections are set up in the app, and Ask IQ chat, payday allocation actions, notifications, biometric unlock, and creating budgets and goals all live there too.
