2. The Adviser Service

The Adviser Service lets an approved adviser:

  • invite clients to connect to them on earmarkIQ;
  • view the information each connected client has chosen to share, such as categorised spending totals and, where the client has switched them on, balances and assets, income and pay dates, and individual transactions;
  • review flagged items and changes in a client's spending over time; and
  • export that information for use in the Firm's own planning and record-keeping.

The information comes from the client's bank connections made through Open Banking and from details the client has entered. It is categorised automatically and may be incomplete, delayed or wrongly categorised. It is not a verified statement of a client's finances and you should confirm anything you rely on with the client.
