What's happening, in brief

Moneyhub has confirmed its consumer app closes on 14 August 2026 as the company concentrates on its enterprise business. For consumers the story is a migration to another aggregation app. For planning firms the story is different: Moneyhub was one of very few consumer tools whose data could underpin adviser workflows, and firms built processes around clients maintaining live bank connections there — whether data reached Voyant through a direct connection, through exports, or through clients reading figures across at review meetings.

Whatever the shape of your firm's arrangement, the common dependency is the same: clients holding active Moneyhub connections. From 14 August, those connections and the accumulated history behind them are gone. The specifics of any firm-level integration vary by agreement, so check Moneyhub's official guidance for your situation — but no firm should assume any client-side data survives the shutdown.

What planning firms actually lose

The expenditure baseline. The quietly valuable thing Moneyhub gave advice firms wasn't the app itself — it was months or years of categorised, real expenditure per client. That history is what let a Voyant base year rest on evidence rather than a client's guess. Once deleted, it cannot be rebuilt; a replacement app starts accumulating from the day the client connects.

The review-time shortcut. Firms using client Moneyhub data at annual review could skip the expenditure questionnaire entirely. Without a replacement, the alternative is the spreadsheet ritual — with all the guesstimation problems that we've written about separately.

Client habit. The hardest thing about client-side data collection is getting clients to adopt a tool at all. Your Moneyhub-using clients had already crossed that hurdle. The migration ask — "please connect your accounts to a new app" — is far easier while the habit is warm than after months of nothing.

The migration path: what to do this week

1

Get clients to export their history now

In the Moneyhub app: Transactions → cloud icon → CSV. This is the only permanent copy of multi-year expenditure history once the app closes. A two-line email to affected clients today protects years of data.

2

Capture expenditure summaries for near-term reviews

For any client with a review in the next six months, record their current category-level expenditure figures while the app still displays them. That preserves a usable base year for the next Voyant update even before a replacement is live.

3

Choose the replacement data source

The realistic options are: revert to questionnaires and bank statements (works, but reintroduces the guesswork), adopt another consumer aggregation app and have clients share summaries manually, or line up a purpose-built adviser data layer as it becomes available. Most firms will run a hybrid for a while.

4

Re-establish client connections before the habit fades

Whichever route you choose, aim to have clients connected to the replacement within weeks, not months. Every month without live connections is a gap in the expenditure record you'll be modelling from for years.
